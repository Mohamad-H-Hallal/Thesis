# TerraLeb compliance engineering baseline

These are implementation targets, not claims of certification or universal
legal compliance.

- Lebanon Law No. 81/2018: counsel-led assessment of notice, purpose,
  minimization, retention, security, rights, formalities and transfers.
- Apple App Review Guidelines/privacy labels/account deletion/privacy manifests.
- Google Play User Data, Data Safety, account deletion, permissions,
  foreground-service and user-generated-content rules.
- GDPR only when territorial/material scope applies; use an approved transfer
  mechanism for applicable restricted transfers.
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and ISO/IEC 27701:2025 control mapping.
- ISO/IEC 29184 notice/consent principles.
- OWASP ASVS 5 and MASVS 2 control verification.
- NIST Privacy Framework and Secure Software Development Framework.
- NIST AI RMF or ISO/IEC 42001 for enabled AI processing.
- WCAG 2.2 Level AA for mobile/web legal, consent and privacy workflows.
- ISO 19115-1 metadata and ISO 19157 data-quality principles for GIS sources,
  derived layers and exports.

Maintain a versioned control matrix linking each applicable requirement to code,
configuration, tests, operational evidence, owner, exceptions and review date.
